# Spec 03 v0.5: G37 and G38 decided (detector CSV cells and rows)

**Why:** the verifier's detector fixtures (#43) found that 03 §8 didn't say how ratios and nulls are written (G37), or which repos get rows (G38). Steven decided both on 2026-10-16, after my review of #43.

**Decisions (03 §5, §8, S15; SPEC-GAPS G37, G38):**
- **G37, changed from the provisional rule:**
  - Each ratio is an exact fraction of integers, for example `star_accel = (30 · stars_7d) / (7 · stars_30d)`.
  - It's written rounded half-to-even to 6 decimal places, always with 6 digits. Null is an empty cell.
  - The verifier's proposal, the shortest round-trip binary64 form, would make two correct implementations disagree in the last digit when they evaluate in different orders. That breaks the byte-for-byte comparison of S12.
- **G38, as provisional:** one row per cutoff and per repo in `C(t)`, including `n_new = 0`; no rows for repos outside `C(t)`.

**Fixtures to update (verifier):**
- D01–D09 ratio cells `0.0` and `1.0` become `0.000000` and `1.000000`.
- My #43 review also asked for three cases:
  - the exact `t − H` boundary (not new)
  - a bot seen only in the label window, by login and by rate over `[t, t + 30d)`
  - a ratio that isn't a whole number
- Until the verifier updates them, D01–D09 don't match the spec. No implementation runs them yet, so nothing fails.
