## G30 (OPEN, raised by the reference agent): JSON text of a non-string `type`

§2 says a non-string `type` "is stored as its JSON text". The two implementations read that differently:
- **pipeline:** the raw text exactly as it appears in the line
- **reference:** `json.dumps(value)`, a re-serialisation

They agree for `[1]` (F22), but differ for `[1,2]` (reference writes `[1, 2]`), `{"b":1,"a":2}` with spacing, or `1.0` vs `1`. That reaches **r2**, a compared output.

**Recommendation: raw text.** It matches how §2 treats `payload` ("stored uninterpreted") and `record_hash` (never re-serialised), and the pipeline already does it. The reference would need a small raw-span reader for the top-level `type` value, about 15 lines using `json.decoder`. A fixture would need e.g. `"type":[1,2]` and `"type":{"a": 1}`.
